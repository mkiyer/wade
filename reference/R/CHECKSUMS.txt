PRUNED 2026-08-18 — the cfRNA consumer layer
--------------------------------------------
Three files were removed from this directory once the Python port was complete
and independently verified. Their digests are retained in the VERBATIM COPIES
block above, so this record remains a complete account of what was staged.

    downstream/wade_contrasts.R
    downstream/wade_figures.R
    downstream/control_strata.R
    downstream/README.md          (written by the R track, not a verbatim copy)

Why they went. They were never porting targets — the downstream README said so
itself — and were staged only as evidence of what a real caller needs from the
API. That evidence has been absorbed: the caller contract they demonstrate is
implemented as wade_contrast(), the nprobs/k arithmetic a caller needs before
running is exposed as tail_window_report(), and the tail.conc display guard they
used at the figure layer is superseded by a guard on the statistic itself.

They also encoded an unpublished cohort's clinical vocabulary — stratum names,
disease categories, contrast definitions — which this repository is intended to
be publishable without.

The five files still listed above are what the port is verified against, and
wade.R remains byte-identical to the cfRNA original. Everything removed here is
recoverable from git history at or before commit ac5c49a.
